Programs

Condensed and clear options so families can choose the right rhythm for their week.

Pricing is kept in one place. Program pricing and package combinations are maintained on the Pricing page so schedules stay easy to scan and update.

Open Play Sessions

Open Play

Daily open sessions with sensory stations, guided free play, and flexible entry patterns for families.

  • Great for day-to-day family time and socializing.
  • Adults can relax nearby and still stay present.
  • Clean setup, family-friendly and easy to book.

Afternoon Hours

Seasonal pop-up afternoon availability for families who need later-day options.

  • Pricing is posted in the latest weekly schedule update.
  • Contact to request new dates.
  • Small groups to keep attention and logistics manageable.

Structured Classes

Music Classes for Babies, Toddlers, and Preschoolers

Music-led movement and language play to build connection and confidence.

  • Drop-in and package rates are available on the Pricing page.
  • Play before/after each class available.
  • Package add-on options are available for recurring families.

Preschool Prep

Semi-structured learning style with creative, developmental activities and interaction routines.

  • Language growth through guided play.
  • Peer interaction and caregiver involvement.
  • Great for families with homeschool rhythm needs.

Baby and Preschool Connections

Baby Connection

Relaxed music and play time for babies and mixed-age families. Reserved to smaller groups.

  • Limited to about 10 families per session.
  • Prepay is required in advance to secure a spot.
  • Active prepaid passes can be used when available.

Preschoolers & Art + Sensory

Tuesdays and special pop-up dates focus on art, bubbles, sign language movement, and sensory exploration.

  • Great for language support and fine-motor confidence.
  • Great mix of process-first creativity and child-led discovery.

Private Groups and Celebrations

Birthday Parties

A complete cozy party setting for ages 1 to 6, with setup support and easy cleanup expectations.

  • Deposits are required to confirm your party date.
  • Works for private and small celebration groups.
  • Gift certificates can be used toward parties.

Character Rental & Private Play Dates

Bring a character experience to birthday events or create private sessions for your group.

  • Private play dates available with a minimum family size for confirmation.
  • Character pricing based on duration and travel distance.
  • Works for homeschool pods, therapist groups, and support circles.

Weekend and pop-up sessions

Recurring and pop-up options are grouped here for easy browsing by season or family need.

Weekend Open Play

Indoor family open play sessions for babies through preschoolers.

  • Weekend rates are listed on the Pricing page with current package rules.
  • Popular formats for Saturdays and Sundays.
  • Great for mixed-age social recovery and new family circles.

Weekend Music + Open Play

Combine structured music movement with open play in one visit.

  • Combined and single-session pricing is published with current weekend availability.
  • Often grouped with family-themed weekend events.

Saturday Family Weekend FUN

Saturday community open play options can include discount passes.

  • Monthly family pass discounts may apply on active dates.
  • Contact in advance for date availability.

Play & Learn Wednesdays

Hands-on weekday sessions with language, movement, and social play.

  • Great for toddlers and preschoolers who like structure.
  • Includes guided parent-led conversation moments.
  • Current timing is announced in direct scheduling flow.

Little Engineers Club

Themed Thursday sessions focused on hands-on trains, crafts, and make-and-take keepsakes.

  • Best for curious learners (ages 2–7 focus, with flexible family joins).

Summer pizza/social pop-ups

Occasional mixed events (open play, music, food, celebrations).

  • Reservations required when event-only.
  • Usually limited by family cap and date timing.
