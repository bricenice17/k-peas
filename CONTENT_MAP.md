## Duplicate and clean-up decisions
- Repeated contact and booking details are kept only in `connect.html` and linked from all pages.
- Payment and policy details are centralized in `pricing.html`.
- Program information is consolidated under `programs.html` by family type, session type, and use case.
- Community support content is consolidated under `community.html` with one card per legacy thread.
