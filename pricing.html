Payment and plan rules

Policy details →

Payment methods

  • Venmo
  • Zelle to kpeasplace@gmail.com
  • Cash accepted by prior agreement
  • No card kept on file

Package usage

  • One music class per day is one package usage unless multiple classes are booked.
  • Packages are family-based and best aligned with routine scheduling.
  • Transfer and date changes are considered case by case.

Refunds and transfers

  • Most plans are non-refundable.
  • Date changes are supported before the confirmed session when possible.
  • Prepaid credit may carry to another confirmed date.

Visit requirements

  • Waiver complete at check-in.
  • All guests follow studio and health standards.
  • Family details should include nap and food timing when needed.
