Payment and flexibility

How I can pay

  • Online card payment or Venmo.
  • Preferred: Zelle to kpeasplace@gmail.com.
  • Cash and Venmo are accepted.
  • No credit card is kept on file.

Plan usage rules

  • A family can include one music class per day in a standard package count.
  • Two classes in one day count as two usages.
  • Prepaid amount can be used within two weeks if schedule changes.
  • Plans are not designed for auto-billing; renewals are manual and friendly.

Policy notes

Refunds and transfers

  • Final sale policy is generally non-refundable unless circumstances are reviewed case by case.
  • Date changes are supported for scheduling reasons before the planned session.
  • Used prepaid credits may transfer only when the team confirms an open replacement date.

Visit requirements

  • All guests complete required waiver paperwork at check-in.
  • No credit card is kept on file.
  • Families should share food, nap, and restroom timing details when needed.
