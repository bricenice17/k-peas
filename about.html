About K Peas Place

An indoor family studio built around safe play, meaningful connection, and simple family decisions.

Why this studio exists

Visit the team →

K Peas Place was created so caregivers can stay close to their children in a playful, structured environment. We combine music, free movement, and child-led exploration with practical support for real households who need clarity and consistency.

Built for family schedules

Daily rhythms plus weekend formats support a range of routines, including busy-caregiver days.

Designed for young learners

Sessions remain intentional, short, and age-aware to avoid overstimulation while encouraging joy.

Community as an asset

Parents and caregivers benefit from local support, event formats, and flexible collaboration.

What to expect on a visit

Check sessions →

Visit rhythm

  • Family check-in is quick and practical.
  • Water access is available; food and snacks are brought by families.
  • Late arrivals are handled courteously with the existing group flow.
  • Caregivers can use adjacent work space when needed.

House style and logistics

  • Sessions stay capped to preserve a calm class environment.
  • Socks are preferred and can be purchased onsite.
  • Families should bring age and napping details if timing matters.
  • Waiver and safety requirements are shared with every booking.

What families keep saying

Every class and open play block is designed to stay simple: children get joy and connection, adults get space to work and recover. That's the core reason we stay in this format.
