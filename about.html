About K Peas Place

An indoor playground and family studio built to help babies, toddlers, and preschoolers connect with purpose.

Our vision in plain words

We exist to create a safe, joyful environment where children can explore, and caregivers can finally sit down and breathe while staying nearby. The space blends play, music, movement, and social connection so your child keeps growing while you build your support community.

Focus on children and families

At the core is fun, but behind every activity are social, language, and confidence-building goals.

Designed for whole families

Programs are intended for babies, toddlers, and preschoolers, with adults invited to join in real time.

Flexible, not rigid

Daily options, package plans, and occasional extra sessions support families with changing routines.

Class and studio expectations

  • No need for perfection; come ready to play.
  • Only water is allowed inside class areas.
  • Only one music class per day counts as one event for package use.
  • Parents can work nearby in designated co-working space when needed.

Safety & care details

  • Please arrive by scheduled start time; late arrivals are handled quickly and politely.
  • Attendance is capped per session for quality care.
  • Families share responsibility for toilet breaks and snack times.
  • Socks are required for adults; socks-on-arrival can be purchased.

Why families keep returning

Our team keeps sessions small and playful, and we intentionally balance structure with free exploration. You get social interaction, movement, music, and community support in one easy place.
